# Operational pain points of Spanish and EU companies (2025-2026) that AI agents could solve

Research date: 23 Sep 2026. Method: web search. Several primary sites (pmcm.es, ec.europa.eu, elespanol.com, finantresnoticias.com, eldigitaldecuenca.com, mifichajelegal.com) were blocked by the network proxy, so some figures come from search snippets and secondary press, not from the primary PDFs. Those figures are marked "(snippet)". Check them against the primary source before quoting them publicly.

## 1. Survey data on Spanish SMEs: bureaucracy, late payments, cash, financing, hiring, digitalisation, AI adoption

### Takeaway
There are two big measurable pains. The first is late payment: the average payment period (PMP) is 67 days against a 60-day legal limit, 85% of large companies pay late, and late payment costs SMEs about €2bn in financing. The second is time lost to bureaucracy: 27.7 h/month per small business owner (CEPYME) and 226 h plus €3,390 per year per autónomo (ATA). Hiring is also hard (45% of firms). AI adoption is still low among small firms: about 21% of firms with 10+ employees use AI, and only about 9-13% of micro and small firms do.

### Cited Findings
**Late payments (morosidad)**
- The PMcM report "Estudio Plazos de Pago en España 2025" (published April 2026) finds that 85% of large companies exceed the legal payment period and only 14% pay on time, compared with 49.5% of microenterprises. The public sector pays in 70 days on average (+3 days in 2025) against a 30-day legal limit. The private sector's PMP is 67 days (+3 days) against a 60-day limit (Ley 3/2004 as amended by Ley Crea y Crece) — [PMcM press release](https://pmcm.es/85-grandes-companias-incumple-plazos-pago-y-financia-parte-costa-pymes-segun-pmcm-linea-advertencias-gobernador-banco-espana/); [Revista Limpiezas summary](https://www.revistalimpiezas.es/actualidad/normativa-limpiezas/informe-morosidad-pmcm-el-85-de-las-grandes-companias-incumple-los-plazos-de-pago-y-se-financia-en-parte-a-costa-de-las-pymes-segun-la-pmcm_20260417.html)
- The press release title says large companies "se financian en parte a costa de las pymes" (partly finance themselves at SMEs' expense), in line with warnings from the Governor of the Banco de España — [PMcM](https://pmcm.es/85-grandes-companias-incumple-plazos-pago-y-financia-parte-costa-pymes-segun-pmcm-linea-advertencias-gobernador-banco-espana/)
- The Banco de España reports a business PMP of 64 days and the PMcM reports 67. In Q2 2025, microenterprises were paid on time on only 50% of their invoices, small firms on 46.8% and medium firms on only 30%. The financing cost that late payment imposes on SMEs is about €2bn (snippet) — [Finantres Noticias](https://finantresnoticias.com/negocios-y-autonomos/morosidad-cuesta-casi-2000-millones-pymes-golpea-caja/)
- The sanctions regime for paying autónomos late is still not in force. Its regulation "podría estar listo en 2026" (could be ready in 2026) — [Autónomos y Emprendedor, Nov 2025](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/pymes/reglamento-que-sancionara-empresas-pagar-tarde-autonomos-podria-estar-listo-2026/20251119125557046617.html)

**Bureaucracy and admin time**
- Small business owners spend on average **27.7 hours per month** on bureaucratic procedures (CEPYME). In Cataluña a figure of 41 h/month is cited. Spain approves 3.8 new rules per day. 60% of Spanish firms rate bureaucracy "muy grave" (very serious) against 25% in the EU (snippet; figures attributed to CEPYME and its 2024-25 reports) — [CEPYME Informe Crecimiento Empresarial](https://cepyme.es/storage/2025/01/INFORME-CRECIMIENTO-EMPRESARIAL_2024_.pdf); [Mundo Emprende](https://www.mundoemprende.com/actualidad/espana-frena-a-sus-pymes-mas-de-20-barreras-burocraticas/)
- ATA (Aug 2026): autónomos lose **226 hours per year and €3,390 per year** to procedures (about 4 h per week in a 47-hour week). The 2025 figure was 200 h and €3,000, so the burden is rising — [Libre Mercado, 14 Aug 2026](https://www.libertaddigital.com/libremercado/2026-08-14/el-gobierno-ahoga-a-los-autonomos-226-horas-perdidas-y-3-390-euros-al-ano-por-la-burocracia-7447113/); [Autónomos y Emprendedor (ATA)](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/info-ata/ata-denuncia-que-autonomos-pierden-mas-3000-euros-costes-tramites-burocraticos/20260810150741055355.html); [ATA 2025](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/info-ata/autonomos-han-invertido-200-horas-3000-euros-tramites-burocraticos-2025-ata/20251219150531047332.html)
- Bureaucracy costs Andalusian autónomos more than €2bn a year — [El Conciso](https://www.elconciso.es/coyuntura/burocracia-autonomos-andaluces-coste-anual_0_2007738952.html)
- Almost half of autónomos work more than 40 h per week, and many work more than 60 — [Autónomos y Emprendedor](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/autonomos/casi-mitad-autonomos-trabaja-mas-40-horas-semanales-muchos-superan-60/20250819115739044887.html)
- Sept 2026, regional press: "La burocracia se está comiendo el tiempo y la competitividad de las empresas" (bureaucracy is eating companies' time and competitiveness), from Cuenca employers. The article could not be read — [El Digital de Cuenca](https://eldigitaldecuenca.com/2026/09/03/los-empresarios-de-cuenca-alertan-la-burocracia-se-esta-comiendo-el-tiempo-y-la-competitividad-de-las-empresas/)
- CEPYME (Feb 2026): 406,000 SMEs closed 2025 with losses, and "el crecimiento económico no se traduce en prosperidad" (economic growth is not translating into prosperity) — [El Independiente](https://www.elindependiente.com/economia/2026/02/06/cepyme-alerta-crecimiento-economico-no-traduce-prosperidad-406000-pymes-cerraron-perdidas/amp/)

**Financing**
- ECB/BdE SAFE survey, Q4 2025: access to financing is improving but more slowly. SMEs reported higher financing costs for a second consecutive quarter. Profits fell (net -6%). "Discouraged demand" (firms that did not apply because they expected a refusal) rose from 3% to 5.2%. SME expectations are most negative on indirect costs (-43.1), financing costs (-31.3), access to financing (-27.4) and **response and processing times (-24.5)** — [Forbes España](https://forbes.es/economia/880595/el-acceso-a-financiacion-mejora-al-cierre-de-2025-pero-las-empresas-alertan-de-que-a-un-ritmo-menor/); [BdE press release](https://www.bde.es/f/webbe/GAP/Secciones/SalaPrensa/NotasInformativas/26/presbe2026-17.pdf); [CESGAR XV Informe](http://cesgar.es/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/XV_Informe-financiacion-pyme_Cesgar-2025.pdf)

**Hiring**
- EBAE (Banco de España), 2026: 45% of firms say labour availability holds back their activity. The peak was 48% at the end of 2025. The share is 58% in construction, 55% in hospitality and 52% in transport — [El Economista, May 2026](https://www.eleconomista.es/economia/noticias/13913909/05/26/espana-tiene-47-millones-de-personas-buscando-trabajo-pero-el-45-de-las-empresas-no-los-encuentran.html); [El Economista, Dec 2025](https://www.eleconomista.es/economia/noticias/13709961/12/25/la-mitad-de-las-empresas-espanolas-tienen-problemas-para-encontrar-trabajadores.html)

**Digitalisation and Kit Digital**
- Official Kit Digital balance (Nov 2025): more than 1.5M digitalisation projects. It claims productivity gains of "up to 65%" and that 12% of beneficiaries doubled their revenue between 2021 and 2023. The report was produced by the programme itself (self-evaluation) — [Plan de Recuperación](https://planderecuperacion.gob.es/noticias/conoce-balance-kit-digital-pymes-autonomos-prtr)
- Frequent criticism: websites delivered were basic, cloned and low-quality, and there were scams by digitalisation agents — [Asest](https://asest.es/story/kit-digitalizador-pymes-opiniones/)
- ONTSI (2025 report, 2024 data): 74.8% of firms with 10+ employees reach at least basic digital intensity. The gaps are talent, training and strategic integration of AI — [ONTSI](https://www.ontsi.es/es/digitalizacion-pymes-2025)
- Aug 2026 press: "sólo el 5,6% [de pymes] alcanza niveles muy altos de madurez" (only 5.6% of SMEs reach very high digital maturity). Article not readable — [El Español](https://www.elespanol.com/invertia/disruptores/politica-digital/espana/20260825/pyme-espanola-avanza-digitalizacion-basica-solo-alcanza-niveles-altos-madurez/1003744361236_0.html)

**AI adoption**
- INE Q1 2025: 21.1% of firms with 10+ employees use AI (+8.7 points year on year). By sector: services 25.7%, industry 17.5%, construction 11.4%. Micro firms (<10 employees): about 13.4%. One secondary source cites 8.7% for firms with 10-49 employees, which is inconsistent with other data (snippet) — [INE](https://www.ine.es/uc/IpFWc4nE); [Thinktur](https://www.thinktur.org/ine-el-uso-de-inteligencia-artificial-y-cloud-computing-aumenta-entre-las-empresas-espanolas/)
- Eurostat 2025: 20.0% of EU firms with 10+ employees use AI (13.5% in 2024). Leaders are DK 42%, FI 37.8% and SE 35%. Laggards are RO 5.2% and PL 8.4%. Spain, at about 21% per the INE, sits near the EU average — [Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20251211-2)

### Inferences
- Late payment is a structural, recurring cash problem that big players create and small ones absorb. A collections agent (automatic reminders, dispute handling, claims under the Ley de morosidad, invoice financing) addresses a pain that hits every week, not once a year.
- The bureaucracy burden is quantified (about 4 h per week for an autónomo, about 28 h per month for a small business owner) and growing. An agent that takes over "trámites" (AEAT, Seguridad Social, notifications from the public administration) has a clear ROI: 226 h at €20-30/h is roughly €4.5-7k per year per autónomo.
- AI adoption among small firms is low but growing fast, so the market is still early. Kit Digital burned trust with low-quality products, and SMEs will be sceptical of vendors who sell "digitalización" without measurable results.

### Gaps
- I could not read the full PMcM PDF: per-sector breakdown, the average collection period (PMC) of SMEs, and the number of bankruptcies attributed to late payment.
- I did not find an independent evaluation (e.g., Tribunal de Cuentas or AIReF) of Kit Digital with post-subsidy abandonment rates.
- I did not find the Spain-specific Eurostat 2025 AI figure broken down by firm size.

## 2. By function: finance, sales, support, HR, legal, compliance, procurement, IT

### Takeaway
2026-2027 brings three compliance deadlines, each with serious fines: Veri*factu (Jan/Jul 2027, up to €50k), digital time registration (decree pending, fines of €1k-10k per worker), and the late-payment sanctions regime (pending). Together they create a forced wave of tooling. Public procurement (licitaciones) is a large, poorly served opportunity: SMEs win only 7% of the money, and 100 firms take 53% of it.

### Cited Findings
**Finance and accounting (Veri*factu and e-invoicing)**
- Veri*factu was postponed by royal decree-law: from 1 Jan 2027 for firms that pay corporate tax (Impuesto de Sociedades), and from 1 Jul 2027 for autónomos and other obligated taxpayers. Invoices need a QR code and the "VERI*FACTU" legend. Using non-certified invoicing software risks fines of up to €50,000 — [Autónomos y Emprendedor](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/autonomos/nuevos-plazos-verifactu-2027-que-autonomos-van-tener-que-cambiar-programas-facturacion/20251230143941047321.html); [Libre Mercado Apr 2026](https://www.libertaddigital.com/libremercado/2026-04-28/verifactu-en-2027-guia-practica-para-autonomos-y-pymes-1b-7395545/); [KPMG tax alert](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/es/pdf/2025/09/tax-alert-verifactu-calendario-adaptacion-sistemas-facturacion-23580700-4.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf)
- The Consejo General de Gestores Administrativos (mid-2026) asks for Veri*factu and e-invoicing to be adapted to the real size of SMEs "para evitar un colapso burocrático" (to avoid a bureaucratic collapse). It calls the model disproportionate — [Datali](https://dataligroup.com/blog/gestores-piden-adaptar-verifactu-factura-electronica-pymes/)
- 63% of autónomos and SMEs surveyed expected to have Veri*factu running from Q2 2026, before the postponement. 36% of gestorías would take a "brief pause" after the postponement — [MuyPymes](https://www.muypymes.com/2025/12/16/mayoria-pymes-autonomos-rumbo-verifactu-2026)
- Secondary claim, unverified: hybrid human + AI gestorías cut management cost by 30% — [AECEM](https://aecem.es/noticias/proyecciones-2026-sector-asesoria/)

**HR (time registration and hiring)**
- The digital time-registration decree was approved for urgent processing on 30 Sep 2025. The Consejo de Estado gave an unfavourable opinion, and as of 9 Sep 2026 it had **not** been published in the BOE. If it is approved in Sept 2026, the obligation would start around Mar-Apr 2027 — [Registrahora](https://www.registrahora.es/noticias/registro-horario-digital-aplazado-septiembre-2026); [ESI Soluciones](https://esisoluciones.es/fichajes/registro-horario-digital-2026-estado-normativa/)
- Planned content of the draft: the record must be digital, personal and tamper-proof, with remote access for the Labour Inspectorate. It bans paper and Excel, requires a trace of every change, and requires 4 years of retention. Fines would be per affected worker, €1,000-10,000. Some vendor blogs describe it as already in force ("RDL 8/2026"). **This conflicts with the other sources, and the vendors have a commercial interest** — [Controllaboral](https://controllaboral.es/registro-horario-digital-obligatorio/); [Factorial](https://factorial.es/blog/nuevo-registro-horario-digital-espana/)
- Three in four businesses expected higher labour costs from the reduction of the working week — [Autónomos y Emprendedor](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/autonomos/cada-negocios-veran-aumentar-costes-laborales-causa-reduccion-jornada-2025/20241226173235040135.html)
- Labour shortage affects 45% of firms (see section 1), with a June 2026 Eurobarometer on SMEs hiring outside the EU — [EC Representation Spain](https://spain.representation.ec.europa.eu/noticias-eventos/noticias-0/el-eurobarometro-destaca-los-obstaculos-y-oportunidades-que-las-pequenas-y-medianas-empresas-2026-06-01_es)

**Sales and public tenders**
- Banco de España (Jul 2026): SMEs receive only **7% of the money** from public tenders — [Autónomos y Emprendedor](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/pymes/pymes-apenas-reciben-7-dinero-concursos-publicos-alerta-banco-espana/20260716123038055001.html)
- 2025: 100 firms, 0.2% of those awarded any contract, took 53% of the value awarded — [Revista Haz](https://hazrevista.org/transparencia/2026/04/solo-100-empresas-concentraron-53-ciento-contratacion-publica-2025/)
- Reasons SMEs do not bid: outdated prices, excessive bureaucracy, short deadlines, lack of staff, cost of preparing the bid, required guarantees and document complexity. Many tenders end up "desiertas" (with no bidders) — [Autónomos y Emprendedor, Sept 2025](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/pymes/pymes-sale-cuenta-acudir-licitaciones-publicas-muchas-quedan-desiertas/20250912152423045453.html); [CNMC consultation](https://www.cnmc.es/sites/default/files/231002%20CP%20PYMEs%20web%20(1).pdf)

### Inferences
- **Tenders** look like the strongest "I need this" candidate for an agent: SMEs have the ability to deliver but not the time to do the paperwork. An agent would monitor the PLACSP, filter tenders by fit and viability (updated price versus cost), read the specifications (pliegos), and prepare the DEUC/administrative documents and a draft technical bid. The pain is quantified, there is little software aimed at small SMEs (inference, not verified), and the payoff is revenue, not savings.
- **Collections plus cash forecasting**: the payment-period data supports a "cobros" (collections) agent that reconciles bank movements against invoices, chases payments, and escalates to a formal claim or the Ley de morosidad route. It fits well with the Veri*factu switch, because invoices become structured data.
- Time registration and Veri*factu are compliance tools that are already commoditised (Factorial, Holded, etc.). The margin for a new agent there is lower; it would work better as a hook than as the product.

### Gaps
- I found no quantitative Spanish data on customer support, legal/contracts, procurement or cybersecurity for SMEs (time spent, costs).
- I found no measurement of hours spent on bank reconciliation or collections in Spanish SMEs.
- I did not check applying convenios colectivos (collective agreements) to payroll as a quantified pain.

## 3. Startups: fundraising, ENISA/CDTI, investor reporting, Ley de Startups

### Takeaway
Fundraising got much harder in 2026. Q1 investment was €731M, down 30%, with pre-seed at zero deals and seed down 34%. Money concentrated in megarounds. That pushes startups towards public funding (ENISA, CDTI), which is paperwork-heavy.

### Cited Findings
- Q1 2026: €731M across 79 deals, -30% in amount and -21% in number of deals year on year. Three rounds of more than €50M took 51% (€370M) — [Funds Society](https://www.fundssociety.com/es/noticias/alternativos/la-inversion-en-startups-en-espana-alcanza-los-731-millones-en-el-primer-trimestre-de-2026-a-pesar-del-ajuste-del-30-del-mercado/); [La Ecuación Digital](https://www.laecuaciondigital.com/empresas/inversion/inversion-startups-espana-primer-trimestre-2026/)
- Seed fell 34% in Q1 2026 and pre-seed recorded no deals. Early-stage investors have toughened their requirements — [Mentorday](https://mentorday.es/noticias/captar-inversion-privada-en-2026-por-que-es-mas-dificil-y-como-prepararte-mejor/)
- Combining ENISA participative loans and CDTI grants with private rounds is increasingly common — [Nexen Capital](https://nexencapital.com/fundraising/financiacion-publica-startups-espana-2026/)
- Q3 2026: three megarounds take half of the investment — [Applicantes](https://applicantes.com/startups-espanolas-q3-2026/)

### Inferences
- Pain for pre-seed and seed founders: finding the right investors and preparing the data room and KPIs for demanding due diligence. It is intense but infrequent (once every 12-24 months) and the market is small; there are many existing tools (investor CRMs, Carta, etc.). A "grants copilot" for ENISA, CDTI, Neotec and regional grants (drafting the application, meeting requirements, justification) may be more frequent and cross-segment, because established SMEs also apply for grants.

### Gaps
- No data on ENISA or CDTI resolution times or application success rates.
- No data on Ley de Startups certification (ENISA) or its uptake.
- No data on time founders spend on investor reporting.

## 4. Large companies: what they buy in AI and what fails

### Takeaway
Enterprise AI is stuck in "pilot purgatory": 95% of generative AI pilots show no P&L impact (MIT NANDA), only 6% of firms are high performers (McKinsey), and Gartner predicts that more than 40% of agentic projects will be cancelled by 2027. In Spain, 84% of CIOs put AI at top priority, but 59% are still at a "development" stage.

### Cited Findings
- MIT Project NANDA, "The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025" (Jul-Aug 2025), based on 52 interviews, 153 surveys and 300 deployments: 95% of generative AI pilots show no measurable P&L impact despite $30-40bn invested. The cause is the approach, not the model: generic tools do not learn from or adapt to workflows — [Virtualization Review](https://virtualizationreview.com/articles/2025/08/19/mit-report-finds-most-ai-business-investments-fail-reveals-genai-divide.aspx); [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jasonsnyder/2025/08/26/mit-finds-95-of-genai-pilots-fail-because-companies-avoid-friction/). The methodology has been criticised (small sample; see [Dave Shapiro](https://daveshap.substack.com/p/they-are-lying-to-you-about-ai-failures))
- McKinsey State of AI 2025: 23% are scaling agents in at least one function and 39% are experimenting. No function has more than 10% of firms scaling agents. 39% report some EBIT impact, mostly under 5%. About 6% are high performers (more than 5% of EBIT). The key differentiator is redesigning workflows — [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai)
- Gartner (Jun 2025): more than 40% of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by the end of 2027 because of costs, unclear value or weak risk controls. It also warns about "agent washing" — [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027)
- Spain, CIOs Disruptores survey (Jun-Sep 2026): 84.4% of CIOs put AI and automation among their top two priorities, but 59.4% say they are still in a development phase. 87.5% of executives are focused on capturing real value from AI — [El Español, 21 Sep 2026](https://www.elespanol.com/invertia/disruptores/grandes-actores/corporaciones/20260921/1003744388333_0.html)
- Grant Thornton: 6 in 10 firms will increase AI investment in 2026 — [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.es/sala-de-prensa/6-de-cada-10-empresas-aumentara-su-inversion-en-ia-en-2026/). Adigital: 89% of Spanish firms expect efficiency gains from AI — [La Ecuación Digital](https://www.laecuaciondigital.com/tecnologias/tendencias/ia-empresas-espanolas-adigital/)
- Firms with more than 5,000 employees: 69% treat AI as a strategic priority — [La Región](https://www.laregion.es/lri/empresas/inteligencia-artificial-lidera-transformacion-empresas_1_20260712-4343190.html)

### Inferences
- Large companies already have budget. What they lack is working deployments that learn from the workflow and measure ROI. A newcomer does better selling a vertical agent tied to one metric (e.g., DSO, time to prepare a tender, hours of document processing) than a horizontal platform.

### Gaps
- I did not find Spanish data on what enterprises buy (knowledge management versus document processing versus legacy modernisation), for example from IDC Spain. The Deloitte "Estado de la IA en las empresas 2026" report was not reviewed in depth.

## 5. Pains frequently mentioned in press and forums

### Takeaway
The recurring pains in the Spanish business press in 2025-2026 are bureaucracy (ATA, CEPYME, regional employers' groups), late payment (PMcM, BdE), Veri*factu and e-invoicing (gestores), time registration (Labour Ministry vs. employers), labour shortages (EBAE) and SMEs being shut out of public contracts (BdE, Jul 2026).

### Cited Findings
- Bureaucracy: [Libre Mercado, Aug 2026](https://www.libertaddigital.com/libremercado/2026-08-14/el-gobierno-ahoga-a-los-autonomos-226-horas-perdidas-y-3-390-euros-al-ano-por-la-burocracia-7447113/); [El Digital de Cuenca, Sept 2026](https://eldigitaldecuenca.com/2026/09/03/los-empresarios-de-cuenca-alertan-la-burocracia-se-esta-comiendo-el-tiempo-y-la-competitividad-de-las-empresas/)
- Late payment: [PMcM Apr 2026](https://pmcm.es/85-grandes-companias-incumple-plazos-pago-y-financia-parte-costa-pymes-segun-pmcm-linea-advertencias-gobernador-banco-espana/)
- Tenders: [Autónomos y Emprendedor, Jul 2026](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/pymes/pymes-apenas-reciben-7-dinero-concursos-publicos-alerta-banco-espana/20260716123038055001.html)
- Labour shortage: [El Economista, May 2026](https://www.eleconomista.es/economia/noticias/13913909/05/26/espana-tiene-47-millones-de-personas-buscando-trabajo-pero-el-45-de-las-empresas-no-los-encuentran.html)

### Inferences
Candidate ranking by intensity × frequency × how poorly served the pain is:
1. **Tender agent for SMEs.** Pays off in revenue, the pain is documented by the BdE and CNMC, and the work (reading pliegos, assembling documents) is text-heavy and suits LLMs.
2. **Collections and cash agent.** Documented weekly pain (PMP 67 days), and it fits with structured invoices from Veri*factu.
3. **"Trámites" agent for autónomos and small SMEs.** Notifications, deadlines, forms; 226 h per year. Risk: it competes with gestorías, or could be sold through them (B2B2C channel).
4. **Grants copilot (ENISA, CDTI, regional grants).** Cross-segment, but seasonal.

### Gaps
- I did not systematically search LinkedIn or forums (Forocoches, r/spain, Emprendedores) for qualitative voice-of-customer evidence. Recommend a follow-up search.
